Project Overview & Objective

The objective of Urban Odyssey was to transform over 180GB of multi-camera 4K ProRes raw footage captured in low-light urban environments into an electrifying 90-second promotional film that commands viewer attention from the first second.

Key Production Milestones

  • 01.
    Beat & Tempo Sync Editing: Meticulously cut clips to audio transients and acoustic cues, delivering snappy momentum without causing visual fatigue.
  • 02.
    Precision Color Science in DaVinci Resolve: Standardized varied camera profiles into a unified teal-and-amber cinematic palette while protecting clean skin-tones and deep black floor levels.
  • 03.
    Layered Sound FX & Foley: Engineered an 8-track audio master featuring spatial whooshes, engine revs, city ambiences, and subtle risers.
